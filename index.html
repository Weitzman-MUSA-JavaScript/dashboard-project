    READ THE DATA CAREFULLY

    What this view can tell you

    01

    Reported activity

    These are police dispatch records, not every incident that occurred. Locations are generalized to the hundred block. A darker tract means more reports under the selected filters, not a higher personal risk.

    02

    Matching comparisons

    The map and detail charts show the selected year. The five-year trend keeps the incident and time filters, and switches between city and tract with the map. Tract populations are context only because residents do not capture visitors or workers.

    03

    Sources and scope

    2021–2025 Philadelphia Police crime incidents, 2024 Census TIGER/Line boundaries, and 2020–2024 ACS population. Read the processing notes.

    This is a historical planning tool, not a live safety alert. Every supplied UTC-year extract misses roughly the last five local hours of December 31. Some geocoded reports could not be assigned to a tract, and geocoding quality varies by year. Homicide dispatch hours should be interpreted cautiously.
