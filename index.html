      About Phlush Finder

      Phlush Finder helps people who are out in Philadelphia find a restroom fast. It answers three questions: which nearby restroom is open right now, whether you can use it for free, and whether it has the facilities you need.

      It is a student project and is not affiliated with the City of Philadelphia's Philly Phlush public restroom program. Hours change, so check with the place when it matters.

      How to read the map

      • Free: public restrooms, libraries, transit stations, parks, and malls.
      • Customers only: cafes, restaurants, and stores. You may need to buy something or ask for a door code.
      • Limited or unknown access: places that need an ID or ticket, or community reports that don't say.
      • Verified hours come from the City of Philadelphia and the Free Library. Other hours come from OpenStreetMap volunteers.
